About Us

Office of the Executive Engineer, Rural Engineering Wing (REW), Pulwama

Welcome to the official web portal of the Office of the Executive Engineer, Rural Engineering Wing (REW) Division Pulwama .

Operating under the umbrella of the Rural Development and Panchayati Raj Department, Government of Jammu and Kashmir , our division is dedicated to transforming the rural landscape of the Pulwama district through robust infrastructure development, grassroots empowerment, and sustainable engineering practices.

The Rural Engineering Wing, Pulwama is committed to strengthening rural infrastructure and supporting the development of communities across the district through effective implementation of rural development works.
